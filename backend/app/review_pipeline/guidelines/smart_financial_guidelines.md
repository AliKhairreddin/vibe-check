# Smart Financial - Compliances

While we value your partnership, we will approach violations with a zero tolerance policy. Our carrier and agent partners require us to uphold strict brand and regulatory standards which we, in turn, expect you to uphold.

If you are found to be in violation of these guidelines the following actions will be taken: Pause campaign indefinitely. Funds will be withheld.

As always, if you have any questions about specific language, creative, disclosures, or landing page requirements, please send them to us for review before launching.

## General Publisher Ad Copy & Creative Guidelines

- **Compliance:** All advertisements must comply with applicable law (e.g., TCPA, CAN-SPAM, state email statutes, Section 5 of the FTC Act, Telemarketing Sales Rule).
- **Truthfulness:** Advertisements should not contain materially false or misleading statements.
- **Clarity of Intent:** Advertisements should make it clear to the average consumer that, by clicking, they will be exploring ways to save on insurance or comparing insurance quotes.
- **Logo Restriction:** No usage of the Progressive logo is allowed in any creative or copy marketing assets unless given specific written consent.
- **Pandemic Language:** No mention of Covid-19, pandemic-related language, or imagery that could confuse consumers (e.g., doctors, lab coats, stock market charts).
- **AI Disclosure:** If the advertisement utilizes AI-generated images or video, a disclaimer must be displayed at the beginning or end of the ad.
- **Currency:** No images of cash or checks may be used.
- **Incentives:** Do not incentivize consumers with discounts, free items, or experiences.
- **Industry Reputation:** Do not use copy that paints the insurance industry or specific carriers in a negative light by creating a false sense of security (e.g., 'Carriers don't want you to know this').
- **Agents/Brokers:** Do not use copy that calls attention or creates a negative perception of the role of agents/brokers; specifically, do not use any assets regarding commissions paid to agents from consumer premium payments.
- **Urgency:** Do not use copy that provides a false sense of urgency (e.g., pricing that has a 'limited time' or 'will run').
- **Inaccurate Actions:** Do not state or imply that an insurance carrier or comparison site has done something it has not (e.g., refreshed rates, re-checked applications, etc.).
- **Pricing Claims:** Do not use copy broadly claiming new pricing for certain states or consumer segments.
- **Vulnerable Populations:** Do not target seniors or other vulnerable populations.
- **Qualifications:** Do not state or imply that the consumer qualifies for some program or savings.
- **Government Association:** Do not imply association with any government program. No images of government insignia, buildings, benefits, or figures.
- **Consumer History:** Do not state or imply something about the consumer's driving record or claims history.
- **Brand Names:** No use of insurance company brand names or logos.
- **Intellectual Property:** Do not use another brand's intellectual property (e.g., Costco, Walmart).
- **Refunds/Rebates:** No refund or rebate language that could mislead or confuse consumers.
- **Approved Copy:** Acceptable creative copy may include: 'Save up to,' 'As much as,' 'As low as,' 'Could save,' or 'See if you qualify.'
- **Sourcing:** Any savings numbers or percentages must be properly sourced, clearly referenced in a disclaimer, and not hidden from consumers.
