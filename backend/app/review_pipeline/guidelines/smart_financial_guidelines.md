# SmartFinancial Advertising Compliance Guidelines for Publishers

These guidelines apply to all SmartFinancial marketing and advertising publishers. The following is not an exhaustive list. All advertising materials must be submitted to your account manager for approval before launch.

SmartFinancial reserves the right to review, request modification, pause, or remove any creative asset that does not comply with these guidelines. Failure to comply may result in removal of non-compliant creative assets, potential termination, and non-payment from participating in the publisher program.

## General Requirements

1. **Compliance:** All advertisements must comply with applicable law (e.g., TCPA, CAN-SPAM, state email statutes, Section 5 of the FTC Act, and the Telemarketing Sales Rule).
2. **Truthfulness:** Advertisements must not contain materially false or misleading statements.
3. **Clarity of Intent:** Advertisements must make it clear to the average consumer that clicking will let them explore ways to save on insurance or compare insurance quotes.
4. **Logo Restriction:** Do not use the Progressive logo or other insurance carrier logos in creative or copy marketing assets.
5. **Brand Names:** Do not use insurance company brand names or logos.
6. **Intellectual Property:** Do not use another brand's intellectual property (e.g., Costco or Walmart).
7. **Domains:** Do not use SmartFinancial or SmartFinancial-owned domains in advertising creative or copy without specific written consent.
8. **Pandemic Language:** Do not mention COVID-19, use pandemic-related language, or use imagery that could confuse consumers (e.g., doctors, lab coats, or stock market charts).
9. **AI Disclosure:** If an advertisement uses AI-generated images or video, display a disclaimer at the beginning or end of the ad.
10. **AI Public Figures/Celebrities:** Do not use AI-generated public figures or celebrities (e.g., Elon Musk, Joe Rogan, Donald Trump, Shark Tank personalities, or Shaq).
11. **Currency:** Do not use images of cash, coins, checks, or money.
12. **Incentives:** Do not incentivize consumers with discounts, free items, or experiences.
13. **Industry Reputation:** Do not use copy that paints the insurance industry or specific carriers in a negative light or creates a false sense of security (e.g., "Carriers don't want you to know this").
14. **Agents/Brokers:** Do not use copy that calls attention to or creates a negative perception of agents or brokers. Specifically, do not use assets about commissions paid to agents from consumer premium payments.
15. **Urgency:** Do not use ad copy that creates a false sense of urgency (e.g., pricing that is "limited time," "expiring discounts," or "will run").
16. **Inaccurate Actions:** Do not state or imply that an insurance carrier or comparison site has done something it has not done (e.g., refreshed rates or re-checked applications).
17. **Pricing Claims:** Do not broadly claim new pricing for certain states or consumer segments. Do not claim that insurance is free, zero-cost, or otherwise misrepresent the cost of coverage.
18. **Refunds/Rebates:** Do not use refund, rebate, cash-back, or monetary-payment language that could mislead or confuse consumers.
19. **Gift Cards/Rewards:** Do not use gas cards, gift cards, prepaid cards, or cash rewards.
20. **Vulnerable Populations:** Do not target seniors or other vulnerable populations.
21. **Demographics:** Do not reference race, age, or other protected groups.
22. **Qualifications:** Do not state or imply that the consumer qualifies for a program or savings.
23. **Government Association:** Do not imply association with a government program. Do not use images of government insignia, buildings, benefits, or figures.
24. **Consumer History:** Do not state or imply anything about the consumer's driving record or claims history.
25. **Approved Copy:** Acceptable creative copy may include "Save up to," "As much as," "As low as," "Could save," or "See if you qualify."
26. **Sourcing:** Properly source all savings numbers or percentages, reference the source clearly in a disclaimer, and do not hide the source from consumers.
27. **Legal Authority:** Do not use imagery of police officers or cars, government buildings or figures, the DMV, or Social Security.
28. **Government Programs:** Do not use government programs, benefits, or assistance programs to imply eligibility for insurance savings (e.g., Social Security, SNAP, EBT, or relief programs).
29. **Language:** Do not use non-English language in advertisements.

## Restricted or Prohibited References

Ads are restricted or prohibited from referencing the following. Failure to comply can result in non-payment of the publisher's account:

- Insurance carrier company names, misspellings, or logos (e.g., State Farm, GEICO, Allstate, Liberty Mutual, Progressive, or USAA).
- SmartFinancial brands, logos, trademarks, or misspellings such as "Smart Financial."
- Other company brands, logos, trademarks, or misspellings such as Costco or Walmart.
- Claims of "free insurance," "zero cost insurance," or similar false claims.
- Claims involving free gas cards, free gas angles, prepaid spending cards, or EBT.
- Misleading language that could confuse or mislead consumers.
- Claims that insurance companies scam or rip off people, or other portrayals of insurance carriers in a negative way.
- AI-generated public figures such as Shark Tank personalities, Joe Rogan, Elon Musk, or Donald Trump.
- Language alluding to a potential refund, cash back, or getting anything back that suggests a refund.
- Absolute savings numbers. For example, "You could save as much as $600/year" is permitted, while "Save $600/year" is not.
- Daily or weekly "rates as low as" claims. Use monthly or yearly increments.
- Cash, coins, or checks to be received by the customer, whether shown in imagery or words.
- DMV images, government symbols or insignia, Social Security departments, or government programs.
- References to demographic groups (e.g., "Savings for Seniors," "Savings for Veterans," race, ethnicity, or gender).
- Non-English-language ads.
- SmartFinancial, MidasRates, or SmartFinancial-owned domains used for Meta pages.
- Images or videos showing personal items being stolen from a car, because auto insurance does not cover stolen personal property.

## Progressive Contactability Violation Notes

### Negative Industry Tone

Prohibited examples include:

- "They've got you paying way more than you need to, when the only thing that actually sets your rate is your car's year, make, and model."
- "Insurance companies often punish loyalty with higher rates."
- "They hide this from US/[State] drivers."
- "For the past 50 years, those companies have spent billions of dollars to convince the rest of us that these big companies are the only option, and that's just not true."

### New Laws

Prohibited examples include:

- "New car insurance crisis rules."
- "Not anymore, because there is a new rule."

### General Unapproved Messaging

- Email creative missing an ad disclaimer.

## Allowed Creative Guidelines and Notes

- "Save up to 50% on auto insurance."
- "Save up to $700."
- Savings claims must be sourced. The publisher may add the source to a landing page or include it in the ad.
- "Could save in new ways when switching auto insurance."
- "Save more when you bundle your auto insurance policy with your home."
- "Depending on your driving record, you potentially could qualify for discounts."
- "New and improved discounts: pay in full, military, multi-car discounts, and more."
- Use "Results may vary" as a disclaimer.
- Prefer "safe driving discounts" over "safe driving program."
