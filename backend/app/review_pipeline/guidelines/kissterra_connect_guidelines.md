# Kissterra Connect Advertising Compliance Guide

This document gathers necessary information to help you promote our offers in a compliant way. These Partner Guidelines are in addition to the provisions of the agreement pursuant to which you engaged with Kissterra.

## 1. General Compliance and Approval Requirements

- All creatives, emails, landing pages, and any promotional materials must receive Kissterra's prior written approval before launch.
- Any new source or channel also requires prior written approval.
- Kissterra reserves the right to monitor traffic and creatives using automated tools as well as manual reviews and periodic audits.
- Violations of these Guidelines may result in rejection of traffic, withholding of payment for non-compliant activity, suspension, or termination of partnership.

## 2. Traffic Requirements

### General

- We operate in the USA only and thus accept traffic from the US domestic market only. NO international geo-targeting.
- Traffic must be from individuals Age 18 and over.
- We can only accept traffic compliant with these Guidelines.
- We cannot accept traffic considered fraudulent (according to industry standards and the Best Practices below). Duplicated/invalid traffic and fraud will not be paid for.
- All clicks provided by you shall be from individual end consumers that have directly provided their information to you in seeking insurance products or insurance information.
- All traffic provided by you must be fully compliant with all applicable laws and regulations, specifically, without limitation, with the Telephone Consumer Protection Act (TCPA), Section 17529.5 of the California Business and Professional Code, and the CAN-SPAM Act.

### Marketing Text Message

- Text messaging requires specific approval from our compliance team. Please contact your partnerships manager if you wish to promote our websites through text messaging.
- You must fully comply with the requirements set forth by the Federal Communications Commission (FCC), applicable legislation, and the following Best Practices:

| Requirement | Best Practice |
| --- | --- |
| Consent | Always make sure you have obtained consent prior to texting. |
| Style | BE clever, engaging, and brief. |
| Keywords | Choose contextually relevant keywords. |
| Frequency | Inform consumers of how often they'll receive text messages. |
| Sender | Always mention the sender's name. |
| Bridge Page | Never direct the link in your text messages directly to our domains. |
| Disclosure | Give notice of potential carrier costs and fees. |
| Opt-Out | Provide consumers with the means to opt out of SMS communications. |

### Creative and Content Prohibitions

- Creative or content which offer any payment, rebate, discount or other monetary incentive to induce a click or otherwise artificially increase traffic is entirely prohibited.
- Creative or content which is sexual, deceptive or illegal in nature or that in any way incites to hatred, violence, discrimination or illegal activities is entirely prohibited.
- Creative or content which refers exclusively to any of the following: military professionals, veterans, specific age groups and/or other discriminatory attributions is entirely prohibited.
- Use of unauthorized language and numbers is prohibited, and will not be payable under the agreement entered between you and Kissterra.

### Spokesperson and Testimonial Rules

- First-person testimonials can be used if the person talking actually used a Kissterra website.
- The amount of the quote/policy can be used, but no brand names under any circumstances.
- "Spokesperson" style is allowed and must carry the disclaimer "Actor portrayal" to indicate that the speaker is a compensated actor and not an actual customer. Spokesperson-style ads must comply with all other content guidelines.
